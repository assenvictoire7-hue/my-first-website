About AgriSHEild AI

AgriSHEild AI is a climate-smart agricultural platform designed to help smallholder farmers, especially young women and girls, adapt to climate change and make data-driven farming decisions.

Founded by Assen Nsongan Gabrielle Victoire, a young Cameroonian farmer, AgriSHEild AI empowers farmers with localized insights on weather, pests, soil health and crop optimization.

🌱

Problem Statement

  • Increasing climate variability (droughts, floods, unpredictable rainfall)
  • Limited access to real-time agricultural data
  • High crop losses from pests and diseases
  • Poor soil management due to lack of diagnostics
  • Gender barriers limiting women's access to extension services
  • Youth exclusion from modern agricultural value chains

Our Solution

AI Climate Forecasting

Hyperlocal rainfall prediction and optimal planting windows.

Pest Detection

AI image recognition to identify crop diseases early.

Soil Health Matching

Smart soil diagnostics and fertilizer recommendations.

Yield Forecasting

Predictive insights helping farmers plan their income.

Expected Impact

  • 10,000 farmers supported
  • 30-50% increase in crop yield
  • 40% reduction in climate crop losses
  • 5,000 girls economically empowered
  • Improved food security for 50,000 people

Implementation Plan

  1. Phase 1: Platform development and pilot testing
  2. Phase 2: Regional expansion and partnerships
  3. Phase 3: National rollout and farmer training
