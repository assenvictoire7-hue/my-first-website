About AgriSHEild AI

AgriSHEild AI is a climate-smart agricultural platform designed to help smallholder farmers, especially young women and girls, adapt to climate change and make data-driven farming decisions.

Founded by Assen Nsongan Gabrielle Victoire, AgriSHEild AI empowers farmers with localized insights on weather, pests, soil health, and crop optimization.
