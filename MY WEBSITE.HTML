Protecting Harvests. Powering Girls. Climate-Smart Farming for Cameroon.

AI-powered decision support for smallholder farmers, led by youth and women.

Executive Summary

AgriSHEild AI is an AI-powered climate-smart agriculture platform designed to help smallholder farmers—especially young women and girls—adapt to climate change, increase yields, and secure stable incomes through data-driven farming decisions.

Founded by a young Cameroonian girl who was forced to drop out of school due to financial hardship and turned to farming to fund her education, AgriSHEild AI emerges from lived experience at the intersection of climate vulnerability, gender inequality, and food insecurity.

By leveraging artificial intelligence, climate analytics, and mobile technology, AgriSHEild AI empowers farmers with localized, real-time insights on weather patterns, pest outbreaks, soil health, and crop optimization, enabling them to farm smarter—not harder.

Problem Statement

  • Increasing climate variability (unpredictable rainfall, droughts, floods)
  • Limited access to real-time climate and agronomic data
  • High post-planting losses from pests and diseases
  • Poor soil management due to lack of diagnostics
  • Gender barriers limiting women’s access to extension services and finance
  • Youth exclusion from modern agricultural value chains

For young girls, these challenges are compounded by poverty-induced school dropouts, forcing many into low-productivity subsistence farming.

Proposed Solution: AgriSHEild AI

AgriSHEild AI is a mobile-first, AI-powered decision-support system that converts climate and farm data into simple, actionable guidance for smallholder farmers.

Core Features

  • AI Climate Forecasting: Hyperlocal rainfall and temperature predictions, optimal planting and harvesting windows.
  • Smart Pest & Disease Detection: AI image recognition via basic smartphones, early warnings to prevent crop loss.
  • Soil Health & Crop Matching: Soil diagnostics and crop suitability recommendations, fertilizer optimization.
  • Yield & Income Forecasting: Predictive analytics to reduce financial uncertainty.
  • Low-Tech Accessibility: SMS and voice-based advisories in local languages.
  • Girls-in-Agriculture Empowerment: Digital training modules, peer mentorship for young female farmers.

Expected Impact (3-Year Projection)

  • 10,000+ farmers onboarded
  • 30–50% increase in crop yields
  • 40% reduction in climate-related crop losses
  • 5,000+ young women economically empowered
  • Improved food security for over 50,000 people
  • Measurable reductions in poverty-driven school dropouts

Implementation Plan

  1. Phase 1: Pilot & Validation (0–6 months) – platform development, pilot with 500 farmers, impact data collection.
  2. Phase 2: Scaling & Partnerships (6–24 months) – expansion to 5 regions, partnerships with schools, NGOs, cooperatives, farmer ambassador training.
  3. Phase 3: National Rollout (24–36 months) – integration with financial and insurance partners, national awareness campaign, policy engagement.
