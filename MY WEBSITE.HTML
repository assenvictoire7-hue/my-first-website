About AgriSHEid AI

AgriSHEild AI is an AI powered climate smart agricultural Platform design to help small holders farmers specifically young women and girls adapt to climate change, Gain stable income and make farm data-Driven decison

Founded by Assen Nsongan Gabrielle Victoire a young Cameroonian Farmer, who faced financial Hardship, AgriSHEild AI epowers farmers with localise,real time insight on wether patterns, pest, soil health and crop optimization, enabling smater farming decision.

<...Problem Statment..>

  • Increasing Climate Variabilty(Drought,Floods,Unpredictable rainfall
  • Limited access to real time and agronomic data
  • High Post plantiing from pest and diseases
  • Poor soil managment due to lack of diagnostic
  • Gender barriers limitting women's acess to extension services and finance
  • Youth exclusion for modern agricultural value chains

For young girls this challenges are compounded by poverty induced school dropout , forcing many into low farming with no pathway to scale.
